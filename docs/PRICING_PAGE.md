# Pricing Page Design & Strategy

## Overview

The pricing page is a critical conversion point for cleaning businesses. It should clearly communicate value, build confidence, and make purchasing decisions easy. Based on modern SaaS pricing patterns.

## Free Trial Strategy

### 60-Day Free Trial (All Plans)

```
"All plans include:
 ✅ 60-day free trial
 ✅ No credit card required
 ✅ Cancel anytime
 ✅ Full feature access"
```

**Benefits:**
- Zero friction to signup
- Users can test all features
- Build trust through experience
- Reduce purchase anxiety
- Drive trial-to-paid conversion

**Trial Features:**
- Full access to all plan features
- At day 55: Email reminder
- At day 59: Final reminder
- Auto-charge on day 61 if card added
- Easy upgrade/downgrade during trial

## FAQ Section

### Common Questions

**Can I change plans?**
"Yes! Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at your next billing date. If upgrading mid-month, you'll only pay the difference."

**Do you offer discounts?**
"Annual plans include a 20% discount. Contact our team for custom pricing on Enterprise plans."

**What happens when my trial ends?**
"We'll send reminders before your trial ends. Your plan won't auto-charge without a payment method on file. You can cancel anytime."

**Can I cancel anytime?**
"Yes, absolutely. No cancellation fees or penalties. Your listing stays live for the rest of your billing period."

**Do you have setup fees?**
"No setup fees! You pay only the monthly subscription. That's it."

**What about refunds?**
"We offer refunds within 14 days if you're not satisfied. After that, we offer month-to-month billing with no long-term contracts."

**Can I get a custom quote?**
"Absolutely! Enterprise customers and multi-location operations can contact our sales team for custom pricing."

**What if I need more than 3 service areas?"**
"Upgrade to Professional (3 areas) or Enterprise (unlimited). You can also list specific zip codes in your service description."

## Conversion Optimization

### CTA Button Copy

**Starter:** "Get Started" (primary-500)
- Not the most popular option
- Standard button style

**Professional:** "Start Free Trial" (accent-500)
- Most popular (highlighted)
- Bold accent color
- "Free Trial" emphasizes no risk

**Enterprise:** "Contact Sales" (neutral-800)
- For custom pricing needs
- Different action (not auto-signup)

### Trust Signals

Include below pricing:
- ✅ No credit card required
- ✅ 60-day free trial
- ✅ Cancel anytime
- ✅ 500+ businesses trust us
- ✅ 4.8/5 average rating

```
⚠️ Early Adopter Pricing
Limited time: Sign up today and get
Professional tier for $49/month (normally $79)

```

## Mobile Responsive Design

### Mobile Layout (< 768px)
- Cards stack vertically
- Full width (with padding)
- Buttons full width
- Comparison table → accordion
- Easier to tap buttons

### Tablet Layout (768px - 1024px)
- 2 cards per row
- Professional featured (left column, full height)
- Cards responsive width

### Desktop Layout (> 1024px)
- 3 cards in a row
- Professional card slightly elevated/larger
- Hover effects on cards
- Smooth transitions
