# Nashville House Cleaning Directory

A local directory platform connecting Nashville residents with trusted house cleaning services.

## Overview

This platform serves two primary audiences:

1. **Nashville Residents** - Find, compare, and book reliable house cleaning services
2. **Cleaning Businesses** - Grow your customer base with qualified local leads

## Value Proposition

### For Consumers
- Find trusted Nashville house cleaning services in minutes
- Compare local providers and read reviews
- Book with confidence—all in one place

### For Cleaning Businesses
- Get discovered by customers actively searching for services
- Qualified local leads without the high fees of national platforms
- Better economics than Thumbtack, Angi, or HomeAdvisor

## Market Opportunity

- Nashville metro area: 2M+ population
- One of the fastest-growing cities in the US
- Strong demand for house cleaning services
- Opportunity to compete with national platforms through local focus
